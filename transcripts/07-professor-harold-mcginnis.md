# Interview: Dr. Harold McGinnis (Professor, Chemistry)

**Interviewer:** Thanks for sitting down with me, Dr. McGinnis. How would you describe your relationship with Compass so far?

**Harold:** Adversarial. [laughs] I'll be honest with you, I was perfectly happy with the old system. It did what I needed. This one requires me to relearn where everything is for no benefit I can identify.

**Interviewer:** Can you give me a specific example?

**Harold:** Submitting final grades. Used to be one page, one form, done in ten minutes. Now it's spread across three tabs — grade entry, grade submission, and grade certification — and I didn't realize entering grades doesn't actually submit them until a student emailed me asking why they had no grade posted.

**Interviewer:** That sounds like it could cause real problems.

**Harold:** It did. I had to go back in, find the "certify" button, which is not where I expected it, and resubmit. Nobody told me there was a second step.

**Interviewer:** Did you get any training or documentation before the rollout?

**Harold:** There was an email with a PDF. I did not read the PDF. I'll own that. But in 27 years I've never needed a PDF to submit grades before, so perhaps that's telling you something about the design.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything about Compass you've found useful?

**Harold:** The lab supply request form, actually. That used to be a paper form I'd walk to the stockroom. Now it's digital and the stockroom manager gets it instantly. Small thing, but I noticed it.

**Interviewer:** What would make the biggest difference for you going forward?

**Harold:** Put grade entry and grade submission back on one page. And maybe don't assume everyone reads their onboarding email.
