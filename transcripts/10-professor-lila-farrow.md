# Interview: Dr. Lila Farrow (Professor, Economics)

**Interviewer:** How has Compass fit into your regular workflow this semester?

**Lila:** Quite well, actually. I coordinate a lot of guest lectures and department seminars, so I'm in the room-booking module constantly, and it's cut my administrative time down noticeably.

**Interviewer:** Can you quantify that at all?

**Lila:** Roughly, sure. Booking a room used to mean emailing the department coordinator, waiting for a reply, sometimes finding out a day later the room was double-booked. Now I see live availability and get instant confirmation. What used to take a day now takes two minutes.

**Interviewer:** Anything that's been less smooth?

**Lila:** The recurring booking feature has a minor bug — if I book a weekly seminar slot and then need to cancel just one occurrence, say for a holiday, it either cancels the whole series or none of it. There's no way to cancel a single instance.

**Interviewer:** How do you handle that currently?

**Lila:** I cancel the whole series and rebuild it in two pieces. Tedious, but only happens a few times a semester.

**Interviewer:** Anything on the teaching or grading side?

**Lila:** Grade entry has been smooth for me, no complaints there. Might be different for colleagues in bigger departments with more sections to manage.

**Interviewer:** Any final feedback?

**Lila:** Just the recurring-event cancellation bug. Otherwise this has genuinely made my job easier, which isn't something I say about university software very often.
