# Interview: Priya Patel (Student, Business, Freshman)

**Interviewer:** This is your first year, so Compass is really the only system you've known. What's that been like?

**Priya:** Honestly kind of overwhelming at first. Nobody really explained how to use it — we got a one-page flyer at orientation and that was it.

**Interviewer:** What specifically was confusing?

**Priya:** The navigation. There's a dashboard with like eight tiles — registration, advising, financial aid, housing, dining, ID card, library, and something called "resources" that I still don't fully understand. I didn't know which one to click for what.

**Interviewer:** Did you find a way to figure it out?

**Priya:** My roommate showed me a lot of it. I don't think I would have found the financial aid appeal form on my own — it's buried under "Resources" and then a sub-menu, not under "Financial Aid" where I looked first.

**Interviewer:** Did you use any of the university's help resources?

**Priya:** I went to the IT help desk once because I couldn't find my class schedule at all — turns out it was under "Registration" but labeled "My Enrollment," and I was looking for something that said "Schedule."

**Interviewer:** That's useful terminology feedback. Anything Compass does well, in your view?

**Priya:** The financial aid tracker itself, once I found it, is actually really clear. It shows exactly what's been applied and what I still owe, which is way less scary than the emails I used to get.

**Interviewer:** Any final thoughts?

**Priya:** I think it would help a lot of first-years like me if there was a short video walkthrough, not just a flyer.
