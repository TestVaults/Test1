# Interview: Callum Briggs (Student, Art & Design, Junior)

**Interviewer:** You transferred in this year, so you've used a different system before Compass. How do they compare?

**Callum:** Night and day, mostly in Compass's favor. My old school's portal looked like it hadn't been updated since 2009. Compass at least looks modern and doesn't feel like it's going to break if I click too fast.

**Interviewer:** What specifically feels like an improvement?

**Callum:** Studio space booking. As an art major I need kiln time and studio hours, and Compass has a visual calendar for that with actual photos of the rooms. My old school had a shared spreadsheet that people would just overwrite by accident.

**Interviewer:** Anything that's been a step backward or just not great?

**Callum:** Transfer credit evaluation is a black box. I submitted my old transcripts through Compass in January and the "status" just says "Under Review" with no timeline. It's been five months.

**Interviewer:** Have you tried to get an update outside the system?

**Callum:** Yeah, I emailed the registrar and they told me it's "in the queue," which Compass could have just told me too, honestly, if it showed any kind of position or estimated time.

**Interviewer:** Anything about the portfolio submission features, since that's specific to your major?

**Callum:** Actually solid. I can upload work samples directly for critique sign-ups instead of emailing huge files around. That's new this year and it's genuinely useful.

**Interviewer:** Overall would you say Compass is better or worse than what you had before?

**Callum:** Better overall, but the credit transfer opacity is a real gap for transfer students specifically.
