# Interview: Maria Torres (Student, Biology, Senior)

**Interviewer:** How has your experience with Compass been this semester?

**Maria:** Rough, honestly. As a senior I have very specific requirements left to graduate, and during registration week the system crashed on me twice while I was trying to submit my cart.

**Interviewer:** Can you walk me through what happened?

**Maria:** I built my schedule, hit "Enroll," and got a spinning wheel for like ninety seconds before it timed out and dumped my whole cart. I had to rebuild it from scratch, and by then two of my sections had filled up.

**Interviewer:** That sounds stressful. Did you get help?

**Maria:** I called the registrar and they said they'd been getting a lot of calls about it that morning — apparently it was a known issue with high traffic at 9am when registration windows open. That's cold comfort when you're trying to graduate on time.

**Interviewer:** Aside from that outage, how do you find the day-to-day experience?

**Maria:** It's fine when it's not under load. I do like that I can see my degree audit and my schedule in the same place now — before, those were two separate logins.

**Interviewer:** Anything about the lab section scheduling specifically?

**Maria:** Lab sections don't show conflicts properly. It let me register for two labs that overlap by thirty minutes and didn't warn me at all. I only caught it because I double-checked manually.

**Interviewer:** What would you prioritize fixing first?

**Maria:** Capacity during peak registration, no contest. Everything else is annoying but survivable. That one cost me actual class seats.
