# Interview: Francine Doyle (Department Administrator, Mechanical Engineering)

**Interviewer:** As department administrator, you probably touch more corners of Compass than almost anyone. What's your overall read?

**Francine:** Mixed, and I mean that sincerely rather than diplomatically. Some parts have made my job dramatically easier. Others have added steps that used to be simpler.

**Interviewer:** Let's start with what's improved.

**Francine:** Course scheduling for the whole department, hands down. I build the master schedule every semester — 40-some sections across our faculty. Compass shows room conflicts, faculty double-bookings, and lab equipment conflicts all in one pass. It used to take me the better part of a week, cross-referencing three spreadsheets. Now it's about two days.

**Interviewer:** And what's gotten harder?

**Francine:** Budget requisitions. That module moved into Compass from a separate finance system, and the approval chain is more rigid now — it forces a strict linear sequence of approvers, but in our department the chair and the associate chair often approve things in parallel, whoever sees it first. Compass won't let the second approver act until the first one does, even if they're both technically authorized.

**Interviewer:** Has that caused delays?

**Francine:** Regularly. If the chair is traveling, everything just sits, even routine things like reordering lab supplies, because the associate chair isn't allowed to jump the queue.

**Interviewer:** Have you raised that with anyone?

**Francine:** I submitted a support ticket in March. I was told it's "a configuration option we're evaluating for a future release," which I appreciated as an honest answer, but it's been a real bottleneck since January.

**Interviewer:** Anything else notable, positive or negative?

**Francine:** On the positive side, faculty can now submit their own supply requests directly instead of routing everything through me first, which has freed up a meaningful chunk of my week. I didn't expect that to matter as much as it has.

**Interviewer:** If you had to pick one fix for the Compass team to prioritize?

**Francine:** Parallel approval routing for budget requests. It's a small configuration change with an outsized impact on how departments like mine actually operate.
