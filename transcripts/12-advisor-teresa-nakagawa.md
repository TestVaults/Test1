# Interview: Teresa Nakagawa (Academic Advisor)

**Interviewer:** You're probably one of the heaviest users of Compass on campus. Walk me through a typical day.

**Teresa:** I'm in the system pretty much nonstop during advising season — back-to-back fifteen-minute appointments, pulling up degree audits, logging notes, checking holds. So I've come to know its quirks intimately.

**Interviewer:** What works well for you at that volume?

**Teresa:** The degree audit tool is a big step up. It flags exactly which requirements are unmet and suggests eligible courses. I used to do that calculation manually with a printed checklist. That alone probably saves me an hour a day.

**Interviewer:** What doesn't work well at your volume?

**Teresa:** Search performance. When I pull up a student's full record — courses, holds, notes, financial aid status — it can take eight to ten seconds to load everything. Across 300 students a semester, that adds up to real time lost just waiting.

**Interviewer:** Have you reported that specifically?

**Teresa:** I mentioned it in a feedback survey in February. I haven't heard back on whether it's being investigated.

**Interviewer:** Anything about cross-departmental collaboration, since you work across three departments?

**Teresa:** That part is genuinely much better than before. I used to need separate logins or spreadsheets shared by department staff to see a student's full picture. Now it's one unified view regardless of which department owns the student's major.

**Interviewer:** Any workflow you've had to invent yourself to work around a gap?

**Teresa:** Bulk actions. If I need to add the same note to fifteen students who all missed a mandatory meeting, I have to do it one at a time, fifteen separate times. A bulk note or bulk email tool would save me real hours every semester.

**Interviewer:** If you had to rank your top three requests?

**Teresa:** One, speed up record loading. Two, bulk actions for notes and outreach. Three, some kind of "flag for follow-up" status I can set that shows up on my dashboard, not buried in a student's individual page.
