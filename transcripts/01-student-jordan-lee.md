# Interview: Jordan Lee (Student, Computer Science, Sophomore)

**Interviewer:** Thanks for making time. Can you start by telling me how long you've been using Compass?

**Jordan:** Since it launched in January, so basically the whole spring semester. I used the old registration portal for one semester before that, so I have something to compare it to.

**Interviewer:** What's your overall impression?

**Jordan:** Honestly pretty positive? The course search is way better than the old system — I can filter by seats available, time of day, and whether it's hybrid. That alone saved me so much time during add/drop week.

**Interviewer:** Anything that's been frustrating?

**Jordan:** The waitlist notifications are weird. I got bumped off a CS elective waitlist and the email just said "status changed" with no explanation. I had to log in and dig through three menus to figure out I'd actually gotten a seat, not lost one.

**Interviewer:** Did you reach out to anyone about that?

**Jordan:** No, I just figured it out eventually. But my roommate almost missed the add deadline because he assumed "status changed" meant bad news and didn't check for two days.

**Interviewer:** How about the mobile experience?

**Jordan:** It's a website that's technically responsive, not a real app, and it shows. Buttons are tiny, and the schedule grid is basically unreadable on a phone unless you rotate it sideways.

**Interviewer:** If you could change one thing about Compass, what would it be?

**Jordan:** Clearer notifications. Just tell me what happened in plain language instead of making me investigate.

**Interviewer:** Anything you'd want the team to keep exactly as is?

**Jordan:** The filtering on course search, for sure. Don't touch that.
