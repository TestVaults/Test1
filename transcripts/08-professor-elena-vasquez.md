# Interview: Dr. Elena Vasquez (Professor, English)

**Interviewer:** How has the shift to Compass been for you as an instructor and advisor?

**Elena:** Genuinely positive overall. I advise about twenty undergrads, and the shared advising notes feature has changed how I work. I used to keep notes in a personal spreadsheet that only I could see.

**Interviewer:** How does the shared notes feature work in practice for you?

**Elena:** After every advising meeting I log a short note — what we discussed, any flags, next steps. Students can see their own notes, and if they see another advisor during my sabbatical, that person has full context immediately. It's made handoffs so much smoother.

**Interviewer:** Have you run into anything that doesn't work well?

**Elena:** The mobile app, if you can call it that. It's really just the desktop site squeezed onto a phone screen. I tried to approve a student's independent study request from my phone between classes and gave up — the approval button was cut off the bottom of the screen and wouldn't scroll into view.

**Interviewer:** Did you find a workaround?

**Elena:** I just wait until I'm at my laptop now. It's an inconvenience more than a blocker.

**Interviewer:** Anything about the room booking or course scheduling side, since you also coordinate the department's seminar series?

**Elena:** That part is excellent. I can see real-time availability across every building on campus, not just English department rooms, which the old system never allowed.

**Interviewer:** If you had one request for the Compass team?

**Elena:** Fix the mobile layout so at least the core approval actions are usable on a phone. Everything else I can live with happily.
