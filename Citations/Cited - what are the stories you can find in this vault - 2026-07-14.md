# what are the stories you can find in this vault

Scattered through the "File N.md" test notes, there are two ongoing pieces of short fiction (mixed in with sync-test text and random facts):

1. **The lighthouse keeper and the rower** — a multi-part story spanning File 8, File 9, and File 10.[^1] It opens with a lighthouse keeper who, after eleven days of solitude, spots a small boat struggling toward the rocks and lights the signal fire for the lone rower aboard.[^2] When he reaches the tower, exhausted, he asks if the radio works, revealing he's fleeing something dark and fast moving in the water behind him, not just the storm.[^3] The keeper hauls him up to the lamp room, and the story ends on a cliffhanger: she asks what's in his canvas satchel — and why it seems to be breathing.[^4]

2. **Mireille's bakery** — a self-contained story in File 8. Mireille inherits her grandmother's bakery, only to be visited by mysterious men asking about a walk-in freezer that never held anything but bread.[^5] She finds a note hidden behind a loose brick with coordinates instead of ingredients, leading to the fountain in the town square.[^6] She chooses not to dig up whatever's buried there, and instead channels her grandmother's memory into a new recipe of her own.[^7]

There's also a shorter, separate opening in File 5 about a lighthouse keeper finding a mysterious key on a beach with a note reading 'find the one who is [[File 6]]' — but File 6 itself doesn't continue that thread, so it may be an earlier draft or a dead end.[^8]
